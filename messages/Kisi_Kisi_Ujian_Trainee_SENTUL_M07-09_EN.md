## MESSAGE 7 — The Divine Dispensing in the Book of Romans (3) — 12 Questions

1. Message: The purpose of the dispensing of the Triune God into the believers. The Sentul instance completed only 3 of the 10 official outline sub-points (I.A, II.A.1, II.B.3) before running out of time — the trainer spent most of the time on a background walkthrough of Romans 1-11, which now also serves as a source of questions (see the warning above).
2. Key terms:
    1. the measure of faith
    2. gifts (teaching/sharing)
    3. peace and joy in the Holy Spirit
    4. conscience
    5. doxology
3. Key verses:
    1. Rom. 12:3-8
    2. Rom. 14:1-3
    3. Rom. 15:7
    4. Rom. 16:25-27
    5. Rom. 1-3, 4, 8, 9-11, 16 (background)
4. Points:
    1. **Point I.A (Rom. 12:3-8, coordination of the members) — 4 questions:**
        1. the measure of faith is apportioned by God (No.1)
        2. the attitude to avoid — pride/self-deprecation (No.2)
        3. the basis for the gift of teaching (No.3)
        4. the limit of the gift of sharing (No.4)
    2. Point II.A.1 (Rom. 14, 15:7, receiving believers) — 2 questions:
        1. the standard for receiving = peace and joy in the Holy Spirit (No.5)
        2. take the conscience of others into account (No.7)
    3. Point II.B.3 (Rom. 16:25-27, doxology) — 1 question: glory to the only wise God (No.8)
    4. **Background material (Romans 1-11 walkthrough) — 5 questions, 1 question each:**
        1. sin causes the loss of the glory of God, Rom.1-3 (No.9)
        2. Abraham as the example of God's justification, Rom.4 (No.10)
        3. child → son → heir, Rom.8:28-30 (No.11)
        4. the unsearchable wisdom of God, Rom.9-11 (No.12)
        5. summary of the Bible — the Word became flesh, then flesh became the sevenfold Spirit, Rom.16 (No.13)

## Study Tips

1. **The background material (10 questions) needs special attention** — don't just read the outline PDF; follow/re-listen to the full recording or session notes of Br. Ronal's Romans 1-11 walkthrough (M07) and "11 problems of Corinth" (M08).
2. Master the ⭐-weighted points first (M07 I.A; M08 X-carryover & the 11-problems background; M09 Romans I & I-continued) — together these represent almost 40% of the 45 questions.
3. Note the Sentul instance's unique emphasis for Romans I in message 9 — not through the "precious stone" (as in the Surabaya version), but through the locality/oneness of the church. Do not study from the Surabaya study guide for this point, since the content is different.
4. Fact-type questions are the most dominant (62%), followed by Interpretation (31%) and Application (7%) — master the specific data (chapter numbers, names, terms) but also understand the meaning behind them.
5. There is only 1 Hard question (M09 No.8, on the veil of glory) — study this carefully, as it is the single hardest question in the entire bank.
