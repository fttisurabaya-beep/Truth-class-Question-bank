```
                                                       Message One
                                                Christ’s Redemption

Bible Readings: 1 Pet. 1:19-20, Rev. 13:8

I.    Incarnation—the first step of Christ’s Redemption.
      A. The Word Becoming Flesh — John 1:14; Rom 3:20.
      B. In the Likeness of the Flesh of Sin — Rom.8:3.
          1. He was only in the likeness of the flesh of sin, not the sinful nature, of the flesh — Num.
              21:6-9; John 3:14-15.
          2. Christ was made sin for us— 2 Cor. 5:21.
      C. Sharing in Man’s Blood and Flesh — Heb. 2:14; 9:22.
      D. Still with the Father — John 16:32.
          1. He came in the Father’s name — John 5:43.
          2. The Father was with Him all the time — John 16:32.
          3. He and the Father are one — John 10:30; 17:22.

II.   Crucified—the second step of Christ’s Redemption.
      A. Bearing Our Sins — 1 Pet. 2:24; 1 Cor. 15:3; Heb. 9:28; Isa. 53:6; Mark 15:25, 33; Matt.
          27:46; Isa. 53:10.
      B. He was made sin for us — 2 Cor. 5:21.
      C. With Our Old Man and the Entire Old Creation — Rom. 6:6; Matt. 27:51; Exo. 26:31; Eze.
          1:5, 10; 10:14-15.
      D. Abolishing the Law of the Commandments in Ordinances — Eph. 2:15; Col. 2:14,.16; 1 Cor.
          12:12; Col 3:10-11.
      E. Destroying Satan and Judging the World — Heb. 2:14; John 12:31.
      F. Releasing the Divine Life — John 12:24; 19:34.

III. Resurrected—the third step of Christ’s Redemption.
     A. Glorified in the Divine Life — John 7:39; Luke 24:26.
     B. Becoming the Firstborn Son of God with Many Brothers —Acts. 13:33; Rom. 8:29; John
         20:17; Heb. 2:11-12; 1 Pet. 1:3.
     C. Becoming the Life-giving Spirit —1 Cor. 15:45; 2 Cor. 3:17.
     D. Breathing into His Believers — John 20:22; 14:16-20.

IV. Exalted—the fourth step of Christ’s Redemption.
    A. He was made Lord and Christ — Acts 2:36.
    B. He was given to be the Head over all things to the church, which is His Body — Eph. 1:22-23.
    C. In His ascension Christ poured out the Holy Spirit upon all His believers—This was the
        genuine baptism of the Spirit that formed the Body of Christ—Acts 2:33; 1 Cor. 12:13.
    D. In His resurrection He breathed the Holy Spirit into His disciples; then in His ascension He
        poured out His Spirit upon His believers. This means that within the believers and upon them
        there was only the Spirit.
    E. Now we are in Him. He is in the heavenlies, and so are we — Eph. 2:6.

Reference: The Basic Revelation in The Holy Scriptures, chapter 2.
```

```
          2. Dibuat menjadi dosa karena kita — 2 Kor. 5:21.
       C. Berbagian dalam darah dan daging — Ibr. 2:14,Ibr. 9:22.
       D. Tetap bersama Bapa —Yoh. 16:32.
          1. Datang dalam nama Bapa — Yoh. 5:43.
          2. Selalu bersama dengan Bapa sepanjang waktu — Yoh. 16:32.
          3. Ia dan Bapa adalah satu — Yoh. 10:30; 17:22.
II.    Disalibkan — Langkah Kedua Penebusan Kristus
       A. Menanggung dosa-dosa kita — 1 Ptr. 2:24; 1 Kor. 15:3; Ibr. 9:28; Yes. 53:6; Mrk. 15:25, 33;
          Mat. 27:46; Yes 53:10.
```
