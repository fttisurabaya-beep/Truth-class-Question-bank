                                                  Message Eight ~ Nine
                            The Son’s Ascension and the Baptism in the Spirit

I.      The Triune God through all the process became the all inclusive and the life-giving Spirit,
        the breath of life, breathed into the disciples:
        A. On the day of His resurrection He came back to the disciples as the pneumatic Christ and He
             breathed Himself into them as the Spirit of life.
        B. He entered into them to be their life, to be the essence of their new being.
        C. This breath, which is His indwelling presence, is life to us; it is our essential being. Now we
             are one with Him in the essential way of life.

II.     The Lord’s training to the disciples:
       A. After the resurrection, before the Lord's ascension, He spent forty days with the disciples, to
           trained them, so they enjoy and be familiar the Lord’s invisible presence — Acts. 1:3.
       B.   The Lord’s invisible presence, more practical, more excellent, more precious, more abundant,
           more real then His visible presence; the invisible presence unlimited by space and time.

III.    The Son’s ascension:
        A. His incarnation brought Him into a new stage, a stage for Him to live on this earth for thirty-
            three and a half years, a stage for Him to be a man living God. His ascension brought Him
            into a third stage. This stage is that of a resurrected man living in the heavens to execute the
            things God determined on this earth. This One in the heavens is the Head.
        B. The disciples they were filled with the divine life essentially, but they were not qualified to
            carry out God's economy.
        C. The resurrected Christ had to ascend to the heavens to be exalted by God, and to be given by
            God the kingship, the lordship, and the headship over all things. He also obtained the throne,
            the glory, and all the authority in the universe.

IV.     The disciples' prayer:
        A. In the forty days of the resurrected Lord's training, the disciples learned the lesson, so, after
            the Lord ascended to the heavens, they could pray together for ten days in Jerusalem in a
            threatening environment — Acts. 1:12-15.
        B. In this ten days praying among them there was no disputation, no fighting, only oneness.
            Before the Lord's death, the twelve were still fighting over who was going to be greater —
            Luke 22:24.
        C. They did not care for their safety or their peace. They cared for the Lord's commission, for
            the Lord's testimony, (Acts 1:8; Luke 24:48), and for the preaching in His name of the gospel
            (Luke 24:47; Matt. 28:19).

V.      The pouring out the of the Spirit
        A. The Head was sitting on the throne in the heavens executing God's administration, and the
            Body, represented by the one hundred twenty, was coordinating with the Head for ten days
            to carry out God's move on the earth.
        B. Then on the day of Pentecost, the Holy Spirit was poured out from the Head and by the Head
            to the disciples and on the disciples — Acts 2:17-18.
        C. This means heaven was brought to earth, and God was poured out on man. This pouring out
            was not essential, but economical. It was not for life, but for power, for administration.
        D. They became not only spiritual persons, but heavenly persons. They became the very joint
            between heaven and earth. They joined the heavens and the earth, and they were cooperating
            with the heavenly Head to carry out God's eternal administration.

      E.    This was the pouring out of the fully consummated Spirit by the heavenly Head, the pouring
            out of the ultimate consummation of the processed Triune God by the resurrected and
            ascended Christ, to accomplish the baptism of His Body in the Spirit.

VI.   The all-inclusive and consummated spirit has two that is essential and economical:
      A. The Spirit that dwells within the believers and the Spirit that descends upon the believers are
          not two Spirits, but one Spirit in two aspects.
      B. From His incarnation through His resurrection, He became this Spirit of breath, which is for
          life intrinsically and essentially. Then after His ascension, He became the Spirit of wind,
          which is for power.
      C. The indwelling Spirit is the resurrected Christ, and the outpoured Spirit is the exalted Christ.
          This Christ in exaltation is the mantle Spirit, which is the Spirit of power and the Spirit of
          authority.
      D. This power from on high was the heavenly uniform to be put on God's chosen and prepared
          people for their qualification to carry out God's administration.
      E. He was breathed into them on the evening of the day of the resurrection as their life
          essentially, and He was poured out upon them on the day of Pentecost as their authority, as
          their mantle, as their clothing, as their heavenly uniform economically.
      F. "Essential" refers to the existence, to the being, and to the life for existence; "economical"
          refers to economy, work, and function.

VII. The authority of the exalted Christ:
     A. In the Son's ascension God exalted Him, and His exaltation includes the highest attainment.
     B. In His ascension He obtained the kingship the lordship, the enthronement, the headship, and
         the glory and honor. All authority has been given to Him in heaven and on earth. All these
         attainments of Christ are not essential for us and have nothing to do with us intrinsically, but
         these attainments have everything to do with us economically — Acts 5:31; Rev. 17:14; Acts
         2:36; Heb. 12:2; Eph. 1:22, Heb. 2:9; Matt. 28:18; Eph. 1:20-21.
     C. Our Christ has ascended and that God has exalted Him. He is the top One in the whole
         universe sitting on the unique throne in the universe.
     D. After His ascension, His exaltation, He poured out Himself as authority on His disciples.
         This poured out, exalted Christ is the authority of the entire universe, and this poured out,
         exalted Christ has become our clothing, our uniform.

VIII. Baptized into the Body of Christ to carry out the divine administration by the authority of
      the exalted Christ:
      A. The breath breathed into us makes us the members of Christ who are so living and full of
           life. But all the members of Christ need to be put together to form one Body.
      B. The baptism in the Holy Spirit is the outpouring of the Triune God, consummated in the all-
           inclusive compound Spirit, by the heavenly Head upon His Body on this earth, not for life,
           but for administration, not as life essentially, but as power economically to carry out God's
           economy.
      C. To be baptized in the all-inclusive consummated Spirit does not only put us, the believers, all
           together to be the Body of Christ, but also positions us to drink Him to receive the all-
           bountiful supply of the processed and transmitted Triune God, that we may be nourished to

           plan — Phil. 1:19.
      D. The resurrected Christ's breathing of the Spirit of life germinates us, making us the members
           of Christ, arid the ascended Christ's baptizing in the Spirit of power, putting us into the
           Triune God, makes all the members of Christ one Body, brought into God's kingdom by the
           Spirit of power poured upon them by the ascended Christ in His ascension to execute God's
           administration, like the one hundred twenty disciples in Acts 1.
      E. This baptism in the Spirit is symbolized by the baptism in water, to be baptized is to be
           baptized into the Triune God, baptism also to be put into Lord Jesus, then in the Epistles, the
           apostle defines baptism. He said to be baptized is to be baptized in the Spirit, which means to
           be baptized into the Triune God. This baptism brings us into an organic union with the
           Triune God — Acts. 9:17-18; 10:44-48; Matt. 28:19; Acts. 8:16; 19:5; 1 Cor. 12:13.
      F. The exalted Christ has been given to be the Head over all things, through the baptism in the
          Spirit, the believers were put together to form the Body of Christ, joined to Him as the Head.
          Christ is the Head and we are His Body, joined together to be the great universal man, that is
          the church. He is also the Head over all things “to the church.” His government is to the
          church that it may be carried out through the church. The Head executes His government to
          the church and through the church to reach every part of the earth — Eph. 1:22.
      G. In Revelation, the all-inclusive and consummated Spirit, becomes the seven Spirits, which are
          the seven lamps of fire burning before the throne of God, to carry out God's administration,
          such a picture shows us that the exalted Christ on the throne as the Head, carries out His
          administration by the seven Spirits as the lamps of fire through the churches as the
          lampstands — Rev. 1:4; 3:1; 4:5; 5:6.
      H. Whenever the saints on this earth, representing the Body of Christ, pray about the situation of
          this earth, the throne in the heavens immediately takes action to perform the things on this
          earth to carry out God's administration — Rev. 8:3-5.

IX.   Preaching the gospel to carry out God's administration
      A. Preaching the gospel is a part of the carrying out of God's administration to spread the
          kingdom of God; this can be done by the people full of the breath within, and filled with the
          wind without.
      B. After the ascension and exaltation of the Savior the disciples went out to preach the gospel to
          the whole creation, the disciples went and discipled the nations not merely by preaching the
          Word, but even more by exercising the authority of the Head, five kinds of miracles
          following the preaching of the gospel by the disciples, this brought His believers into the
          foretaste of the works of power of the coming age — Mark. 16:15.
      C. The proper understanding of the casting out of demons and of the speaking of new tongues is
          to realize that they are related to God's governmental administration. All of the items listed in
          Mark 16:17-18 are the display of the governmental authority of Christ's administration.

X.    The Need of an appropriate understanding and application
      A. All the things mentioned in Mark 16, is about the Lord poured Himself out as the Spirit of
          economy upon His believers, a new age began; from the ascension of Christ up until today,
          here and there, now and then, new tongues were spoken, illnesses were healed, demons were
          cast out, and dead people were raised up.
      B. At the time of Pentecost and in Peter's ministry there were miracles of healing and even of
          resurrection (Acts 3:6-8; 9:32-42). In the earlier stage of the Apostle Paul's ministry there
          were also healing miracles. But in the later stage of Paul's ministry he charged his intimate
          co-worker, Timothy, to use a little wine for the sake of his stomach and frequent weaknesses
          — 1 Tim. 5:23.
      C. Paul and his co-workers were under the exercise of the inner life in this time of suffering
          rather than under the power of the outward gift. The former is of grace in life; the latter of
          gift in power—miraculous power.
      D. I hope this kind of word rescues us from our natural concepts, in the decline of the church
          and in suffering for the church, the gift of power is not as much needed as the grace in life.
          Too much miraculous healing distracts people from the inner life.

XI.   The taste of the work of power of the coming age:
      A. Two kind enjoyment of Christ:
          1. The good Word, is the beginning of the teaching of Christ, the good word refers to Christ
              in His earthly ministry, including His incarnation, His human living, His crucifixion, and
              His resurrection — Heb. 6:1.
          2. The teaching of righteousness refers to Christ in His heavenly ministry, including His
              ascension, His exaltation, and all His attainments and obtainments in His exaltation —
              Heb. 5:13.
      B. The second enjoyment
          1. For God to carry out His New Testament economy, there is also the need of the
              miraculous works, miracles, such as healing the diseases, casting out the demons,
              swallowing up the death poison, controlling the Devil, which is signified in the picking
                up of the serpents, and speaking in tongues to diminish the differences, the separations,
                and the divisions of mankind.
            2. Today in the church life we can have such a foretaste. Satan and the demons have no
                ground among us, and the language barrier is no longer a problem. It has been overcome
                by all speaking the same word of Christ—the word concerning Christ. Also, we are
                being healed, and the death poison is being removed, the believers, who are genuinely
                one with the exalted Christ, have been brought into a foretaste of the coming age.
      C.    Due to our natural understanding and our natural desire, we treasure this kind of taste of the
            miraculous things more than the marvelous earthly ministry and the excellent heavenly
            ministry of the all-inclusive Christ for our enjoyment today. So we must enjoy the all
            inclusive Christ as the blessing of the New Testament.

XII. The New Testament Blessing:
     A. Regeneration — John 3:3, 6; 1 Pet. 1:23.
     B. Divine nature and divine life — 1 John 5:11-12; 2 Pet. 1:4.
     C. The indwelling Spirit — Rom. 8:9, 16.
     D. Transformation — 2 Cor. 3:18.
     E. Be mingled with God and can join to Him as one — 1 Cor. 6:17.
     F. Shall be like God and have the same image as God, to enter into God's glory — 1 John 3:2; 2
         Cor. 3:18; 1 Pet. 5:10.

XIII. The content of God’s New Testament Economy—A Person:
      A. The content of God's New Testament economy is a Person who is both divine and human, a
          Person who is both God and man.
      B. He has passed through incarnation, human living, crucifixion, resurrection, and ascension
          and has consummated in the all-inclusive, processed, compound, life-giving, and indwelling
          Spirit imparted into us in His resurrection as life to us, and poured out upon us in His
          ascension as power to us that we may live the divine life to express God and move with the
          divine power, economically, to carry out God's economy.

XIV. We must be those enjoying the New Testament blessings and, if the Lord wills, we will also
     enjoy the subsidiary blessings which are the works of power of the age to come, we should
     focus on the New Testament blessings, which are the main blessings.

XV. God’s New Testament Economy concerning the Spirit in The Acts
    A. The baptism in the Spirit:
        1. One happened on the day of Pentecost when the one hundred twenty were baptized —
             Acts. 2:1-4.
        2. And the other was in the house of Cornelius — Acts 10:24, 44-46.
        3. The Head, Christ, accomplished the baptism in the Spirit on His entire Body. All the
             Jewish believers need to realize that they were baptized in the Spirit on the day of
             Pentecost with Peter and the one hundred twenty. The Gentile believers must realize that
             they were all baptized in the house of Cornelius.
    B. The outward filling and inward filling
        1. Being filled outwardly (Gk. pletho), being filled inwardly (pleroo), become full (pleres).
        2. Being filled by the Spirit outwardly (Gk. pletho) and receiving the power are for the
             work. Being filled by the Spirit inwardly (pleroo) is for life. Become full of the Spirit
             inwardly (pleres) is for life — Luke 24:49; Acts 1:5-8; 13:52; 6:3, 5; 7:55; 11:24.
    C. The leading of the Spirit — Acts. 8:29, 39; 13:2; 15:28; 16:6-7; 20:23, 28; 14:23; 21: 4, 11:
        The apostles and the disciples in Acts were people living, moving, working, laboring,
        traveling, and doing everything by the leading of the Spirit.
    D. These are the members of the Body of Christ, inwardly they were filled with the Spirit of life
        essentially, outwardly they were filled with the Spirit of power economically, as a result they
        are belong to the Spirit and become full of Spirit, living and moving on this earth for the
        testimony of the resurrected and exalted Christ and the fulfillment of God's plan according to
        His eternal economy.

         tahta, untuk melaksanakan administrasi Allah. Lukisan ini memperlihatkan kepada kita:
         Kristus yang ditinggikan di atas takhta sebagai Kepala, adalah melalui gereja sebagai kaki
         dian, berdasarkan tujuh Roh sebagai obor untuk melaksanakan administrasi Allah — Why.
         1:4; 3:1; 4:5; 5:6.
      H. Setiap kali kaum saleh di atas muka bumi mewakili Tubuh Kristus, berdoa bagi peristiwa-
         peristiwa di muka bumi, takhta surga segera melakukan pergerakan, di muka bumi
         menggenapkan perkara, untuk melaksanakan administrasi Allah — Why 8:3-5.

IX.   Mengabarkan Injil dan melaksanakan administrasi Allah:
      A. Mengabarkan Injil adalah satu bagian dalam melaksanakan administrasi Allah untuk
         memperluas kerajaan Allah; ini dikerjakan melalui sekelompok orang yang di dalamnya
         penuh dengan “udara”, dan di luarnya penuh dengan “angin”.
      B. Setelah Juruselamat terangkat dan ditinggikan, para murid pergi keluar mengabarkan Injil
         kepada semua makhluk ciptaan, para murid tidak hanya mengabarkan firman Tuhan, terlebih
         lagi melaksanakan kuasa dari Sang Kepala. Lima mujizat yang menyertai para murid dalam
         mengabarkan Injil, sepenuhnya adalah ekspresi pergerakan kuasa dari Sang Kepala, agar
         kaum beriman mencicipi kuasa di masa yang akan datang — Mrk. 16:15.
      C. Pengertian yang tepat mengenai mengusir setan, berbicara dalam bahasa-bahasa yang baru
         dan beberapa mujizat lainnya yang dipaparkan dalam Injil Markus, adalah mengenal hal-hal
         tersebut berhubungan dengan pengaturan administrasi Allah, itu semua menyatakan kuasa
         pengaturan administrasi Kristus.

X.    Memerlukan pengertian dan pelaksanaan yang tepat:
      A. Hal yang dibahas dalam Markus 16, adalah mengacu kepada Tuhan mencurahkan diri-Nya
         sendiri sebagai Roh ekonomikal ke atas para murid, suatu zaman yang baru telah dimulai;
         yaitu dari keterangkatan Kristus hingga hari ini, setiap waktu dan dimana-mana ada orang
         yang berbahasa lidah, menyembuhkan penyakit, dan membangkitkan orang mati.
      B. Pada saat Pentakosta, saat Petrus menunaikan ministri, dan awal Paulus menunaikan ministri
         semuanya melakukan mujizat-mujizat ini, tetapi pada akhir ministri Paulus, dia menasihati
         Timotius minum sedikit anggur untuk menjaga sakit lambungnya, dia malah tidak
         menggunakan karunianya untuk menyembuhkan penyakit — Kis. 3:6-8; 9:32-42; 19:12; 1
         Tim. 5:23.
      C. Maksudnya adalah pada saat Paulus dan sekerjanya mengalami penderitaan, adalah
         menggunakan hayat yang di dalam, dan bukan menggunakan kuasa karunia yang di luar; yang
          pertama adalah anugerah dari hayat, yang kedua adalah karunia dari kuasa mujizat.
      D. Mohon Tuhan menyelamatkan kita terlepas dari pemikiran yang alamiah. Pada saat gereja
         merosot dan gereja mengalami penderitaan, kuasa karunia tidak terlalu dibutuhkan seperti
         anugerah hayat; terlalu banyak kuasa karunia mujizat, malah membuat manusia terpisah dari
         hayat yang di dalam.

XI.   Mencicipi kuasa di masa yang akan datang:
      A. Dua jenis kenikmatan terhadap Kristus:
         1. Firman yang baik, adalah permulaan dari ajaran tentang Kristus, mengacu kepada ministri
            Kristus di muka bumi, yang mencakup inkarnasi-Nya, penghidupan insani-Nya,
            ketersaliban-Nya dan kebangkitan-Nya — Ibr. 6:1.
         2. Ajaran tentang kebenaran mengacu kepada ministri surgawi Kristus, mencakup
            keterangkatan-Nya, peninggian-Nya, dan segala hal yang dicapai dan didapat pada saat
            ditinggikan — Ibr. 5:13.
      B. Kenikmatan yang kedua :
         1. Untuk mewujudkan ekonomi Perjanjian Baru-Nya, dibutuhkan mujizat, seperti yang
              dicatat dalam Injil Markus pasal 16, yaitu menyembuhkan penyakit, mengusir setan,
              meminum racun yang mematikan namun tidak mendapat celaka, berkuasa atas Iblis
              (dilambangkan oleh tangan yang dapat memegang ular), berbicara bahasa-bahasa yang
              baru (menghapus perbedaan, jarak dan perpecahan umat manusia).
         2. Di dalam penghidupan gereja hari ini kita dapat memiliki pencicipan seperti ini : Satan
              dan Iblis tidak memiliki kedudukan di tengah-tengah kita, perkara perbedaan bahasa juga
              tidak akan menjadi masalah lagi—kita bersama-sama membicarakan Kristus, unsur racun
              yang mematikan telah disingkirkan—mendapat kesembuhan, kita telah dibawa kepada
              pencicipan di masa yang akan datang.
      C. Karena pengertian kita yang alamiah, kita terlalu memustikakan kuasa di masa yang akan
         datang (perkara-perkara mujizat), dan mengabaikan kenikmatan akan Kristus yang Almuhit,
         itu barulah kenikmatan utama pada zaman Perjanjian Baru.

XII. Berkat dalam Perjanjian Baru :
     A. Kelahiran kembali — Yoh. 3:3, 6; 1 Ptr. 1:23.
      B. Hayat ilahi dan sifat ilahi — 1 Yoh. 5:11-12; 2 Ptr. 1:4.
      C. Roh yang berhuni — Rm. 8:9, 16.
      D. Diubah — 2 Kor. 3:18.
      E. Bersatu dan berbaur dengan Tuhan — 1 Kor. 6:17.
      F. Seperti Allah, masuk ke dalam kemuliaan Allah — 1 Yoh. 3:2; 2 Kor. 3:18; 1 Ptr. 5:10.

XIII. Isi dari Ekonomi Allah—Satu Manusia :
      A. Dia adalah yang ilahi, juga adalah yang insani; Dia adalah Allah, juga adalah manusia.
      B. Dia telah berinkarnasi, menempuh penghidupan insani, mati disalib, bangkit dan terangkat,
         serta telah rampung menjadi Roh yang Almuhit, melalui proses, majemuk, pemberi-hayat dan
         berhuni, lalu disalurkan ke dalam kita menjadi hayat kita, kemudian di dalam kebangkitan
         dicurahkan di atas kita menjadi kuasa bagi kita, sehingga kita dapat memperhidupkan hayat
         Allah dan mengekspresikan Dia, serta secara ekonomikal bergerak dengan membawa kuasa
         untuk melaksanakan ekonomi-Nya.

XIV. Kita harus menjadi sekelompok orang yang menikmati berkat dalam Perjanjian Baru, jika
     Tuhan memberikan, kita juga dapat sedikit menikmati berkat tambahan—kuasa di masa
     yang akan datang. Namun kita harus menikmati dan memperhatikan berkat dalam
     Perjanjian Baru.

XV. Ekonomi Perjanjian Baru Allah mengenai Roh itu di dalam Kisah Para Rasul :

      A. Baptisan di dalam Roh itu :
          1. Satu kali terjadi pada hari Pentakosta, pada hari itu ada 120 orang yang menerima
             baptisan Roh itu — Kis. 2:1-4.
          2. Kali lain adalah di rumah Kornelius — 10:24, 44-46.
          3. Kristus Sang Kepala melalui dua kali baptisan itu telah merampungkan baptisan Roh itu
             di atas seluruh Tubuh-Nya. Seluruh kaum beriman Yahudi harus melihat bahwa pada hari
             Pentakosta itu mereka beserta Petrus dan 120 orang lainnya bersama-sama telah menerima
             baptisan Roh itu; seluruh kaum beriman kafir juga harus melihat bahwa mereka di rumah
             Kornelius telah menerima baptisan Roh.
      B. Dipenuhi secara luaran dan dipenuhi secara batiniah :
         1. Dipenuhi secara luaran (pletho), dipenuhi secara batiniah (pleroo), penuh (pleres).
          2. Pemenuhan oleh Roh itu secara luaran (pletho) dan menerima kuasa adalah untuk
             pekerjaan. Pemenuhan oleh Roh itu secara batiniah (pleroo) adalah untuk hayat. Di dalam
             penuh dengan Roh itu (pleres) adalah untuk hayat — Luk. 24:49; Kis. 1:5-8; 13:52; 6:3, 5;
             7:55; 11:24.
      C. Pimpinan Roh itu — Kis. 8:29, 39; 13:2; 15:28; 16:6-7; 20:23, 28; 14:23; 21: 4, 11:
             Para rasul dan para murid berdasarkan pimpinan Roh dalam menempuh penghidupan,
         bergerak, bekerja, berjerih lelah, melakukan perjalanan, bahkan dalam mengerjakan segala
         sesuatu.
      D. Seluruh anggota Tubuh Kristus, mereka secara batiniah dan secara esensial dipenuhi oleh Roh
         hayat; secara luaran dan secara ekonomikal dipenuhi oleh Roh kuasa, dan menjadi manusia
         yang merupakan milik Roh dan penuh dengan Roh, yang hidup dan bergerak di muka bumi,
         bagi kesaksian Kristus yang bangkit dan ditinggikan, dan berdasarkan Ekonomi kekal Allah
         merampungkan rencana Allah.
