## Points taught

1. **Transition into message 6's thesis: after the previous messages covered the Triune God dispensing Himself into believers, message 6 covers the glorious results of this divine dispensing, focusing on how the divine Trinity as the law of the spirit of life operates within believers.**
   > "We come to message 6... The title of this message is the results of the dispensing of the divine trinity as the law of the spirit of life in the believers... in message six, we will see the glorious results of this divine dispensing... Specifically focusing on how the divine in Trinity as the law of the spirit of life operates within the believers."

2. **Recap-into-thesis: the Triune God passed through incarnation, human living, death and resurrection to become the life-giving Spirit, and now operates as the law of the spirit of life, dispensing into every part of the tripartite being (spirit, soul, body).**
   > "He has come into our being and is now working in us, operating us as law of the spirit of life and he is dispensing as the law into every part of our being, the tripartite being, right? Spirit, soul and our body."

3. **God's goal is not merely His own life but His divine life added into and joined with our human life — illustrated by grafting a good peach tree branch into a poor peach tree, producing a union that bears large, sweet fruit.**
   > "What God wants is not simply his own life, but his own life, his divine life added into our human life. So these two lives, divine life and our human life are coming together. It is like grafting, right?... So, if we graft the branches of the good tree into the poor peach tree... large and sweet pitches will be produced."

4. **The highest standard of Christian living is to live the mingled life of a "God-man" — far higher than mere moral, ethical, or outwardly improved living.**
   > "So, the highest standard of living for a Christian is to live this mingle life of a God man, not just live live a moral life, not just live a ethical life, not just live a, you know, good life... This is far higher than merely having good behavior. This is far higher than striving for outward improvement."

5. **This grafted life is realized when we call on the Lord's name and His holy breath enters us — but living it out requires our natural life to be subdued rather than us striving to improve ourselves.**
   > "When the Lord becomes the life-giving spirit, he breathe himself into us and we call on his name. His holy breath is enter into us and he is now grafted into us to be a mingling of the two lives... Our natural effort, our natural desire need to be subdued... We should not strive or we should not struggle to improve ourselves. We just we need to simply hand ourselves over to this operating law."

6. **[Outline I] The result of the divine dispensing: Romans says believers being saved in the life of Christ that they may reign in this life.**
   > "So Roman 1 says, 'The believers being saved in the life of Christ that they may reign in in this life.' This is a wonderful result of the divine dispensing of the Triune God as the law of the spirit of life."

7. **The book "Salvation in Life" (Brother Lee) describes a watershed in Romans at Romans 5:10, dividing the book into two sections: judicial redemption (by Christ's death) and organic salvation (carried out by Christ's life).**
   > "Brother Lee said... 'There's a water shed in the book of Romans.'... This water shed is Romans 5:10... 'we will be saved in his life, having been reconciled.'... reconciliation is the result of the judicial redemption... having been uh reconciled, we much more we will be saved in his life. So, we salvation salvation in life."

8. **[Outline I.A] The result of enjoying the divine dispensing as the operating law in our spirit is not merely being justified or reconciled, but being saved in Christ's life.**
   > "So, what is the result of our enjoying of the divine dispensing of the divine Trinity as the operating law is all in our spirit. It is not just justified, not just reconciled, no. We need to we are being saved in the Christ life. Not only being saved, but also to life."

9. **This salvation in life delivers believers from (at least) six negative things — introduced as a list before being unpacked one by one.**
   > "And this salvation in life saves us delivers us from at least this following six negative things. This is the aspects of our being saved in this life."

10. **[I.A.1] Freed from the law of sin and of death — this law entered the fallen flesh at the fall and automatically operates there, empowering sin and weakening us toward good; the law of the spirit of life delivers us from it.**
    > "Number one, to be freed from the law of sin and of death... this law is called the law of sin and of death... this law of sin and of death always conquer us, captivates us... praise the Lord. We are being saved by Christ's life. We are being delivered."

11. **[I.A.2] Saved from the body of this death — the fallen body, called both "the body of sin" (strong to transgress) and "the body of this death" (weak in doing what pleases God).**
    > "Number two, to be saved from the body of this death... So sin empowers us... to commit sin, to commit transgression, but death makes us the the corrupted body completely weakened, powerless, making whatever we do dead."

12. **[I.A.3] Saved from the mortality of the body — illustrated by a $100 deposit spent down $1 a year, describing the fallen body's aging, sickness, and dying.**
    > "Number three, to be saved from the mortality of the body... It is like having a deposit of $100... we spend $1 every year... What is it? This is mortality... the law of the spirit of life saves us from mortality of our fallen body."

13. **[I.A.4] Saved from being common — the fallen nature's unconquerable love of worldliness and being fashioned according to the age (Rom 12); only living by the law of the spirit of life gradually removes the taste for common things.**
    > "For to be saved from being common... every fallen being, there is a love for worldliness... unconsciously we become common... Paul told us in Romans 12, he said, 'Do not be fashion according to this age.'... only by living according to the law of the spirit of life, can we be gradually saved from all common things and all worldly thoughts."

14. **[I.A.5] Saved from a low manner of living — Romans 12:9-15 describes the high standard of living (love, honor, hospitality, blessing persecutors, rejoicing/weeping with others) that results from the law of the spirit of life operating.**
    > "Number five, it says, to be saved from a low manner of living... Romans 12:10... love one another, lovingly and brotherly love. Take the lead in showing honor to one another. Verse 13, contribute to the knees of the saints, pursue hospitality... Verse 14 Bless those who persecute you... Verse 15, rejoice with those who rejoice weep with those who weep."

15. **[I.A.6] Saved from division — Romans 16:17 tells believers to mark and turn away from those who cause divisions; only being in the spirit and going along with this law lets believers receive one another and maintain the oneness of the Body.**
    > "Number six, To be saved from division... Romans 16:17 told tells us that make mark those who make the visions and causes of stumbling and turn away from them... only by being in the spirit, only by go along with the law of the spirit of life, we can receive others from the depths of our heart... we can maintain the oneness of the body of Christ."

16. **[Outline I.B] The law of the spirit of life not only delivers negatively but also causes believers to reign in life — introducing the sub-points of reigning.**
    > "Next point is B, the believers raining in the life of Christ. So, the law of the spirit of life not only saves us and delivers us from negative things, but also enables us to rain in life."

17. **[I.B.1] Subduing all people, events, and things that oppose God's righteousness, holiness, and glory.**
    > "Number one, subduing all the people, events, and things that oppose God's righteousness, holiness, and glory... this law subdues anything contrary to God's righteousness, holiness, and glory... This law can enables us to express God in his glory."

18. **[I.B.2] Subduing God's enemy Satan, crushing him under our feet (Romans 16:20) — a matter of God's dispensing as we enjoy it in the church.**
    > "Number two, subduing God's enemy Satan... Romans 16:20 it says, 'Now the God of peace will crush Satan under your feet shortly.'... even the crushing of Satan under our feet is a matter of God's dispensing... We can crush Satan on our feet by by enjoying the law of the spirit of life."

19. **[I.B.3] Bringing in God's kingdom (Romans 14:17) — the kingdom of God being righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.**
    > "Number three, bringing God's kingdom. Romans 14:17. For the kingdom of God is not eating and drinking but righteousness and peace And the joy in the Holy Spirit... this is the reality of the kingdom of God."

20. **[Outline II] Every part of the believers being sanctified — not merely positional but dispositional sanctification, through saturation of the whole being with the holy nature of the law of the spirit of life.**
    > "Okay, Roman 2. It says, every part of the believers being sanctified... this sanctification is not merely positional. This is a dispositional sanctification... through the saturation of our entire being with the holy nature of the divine working as the law of the spirit of life."

21. **The sanctifying work of the spirit has three steps: (1) seeking and convicting sanctification before salvation (1 Peter 1:2); (2) sanctifying us both positionally (by the blood, Heb 13:12) and dispositionally (in the Spirit at regeneration, 1 Cor 6:11); (3) sanctifying us dispositionally as we pursue growth in life (Romans 6:19, 22).**
    > "The sanctification by the spirit is of three steps. The first, the spirit seeking us and and convicting us... First Peter chapter 1:2... this is the seeking sanctification... And number two, the spirit sanctifying us both aspects positional aspect and dispositional sanctification... Hebrews 13:12... And 1 Corinthians 6:11... And step three, number third, spirit-sanctifying work us dispositionally as we pursue the growth in life... Romans 6:19 and 22."

22. **[Outline III] The believers' mind being renewed — the renewing of the mind is the foundation for the transformation of the soul, and results from setting the mind on the spirit; the new element (God Himself) discharges the old element and replaces it, producing a metabolic change.**
    > "Okay, Romans 3. The believers' mind being renewed... The renewing of the mind is the foundation for the transformation of our soul... how can be our mind renewed? It is the result of our setting our mind on the on the spirit... The new element that is God himself is being added in us and discharging the old element in our mind and replace it."

23. **[Outline IV] Every part of the believer's soul being transformed — mind, emotion, and will; the mind is renewed first because it is the leading part of the soul, and emotion and will follow; transformation is an inward metabolic process spreading life into the entire soul.**
    > "Roman 4, every part of the believer soul being transformed. So, every part of our soul, our mind, our emotion, and our will. First, mind need to be renewed because mind is the leading part of our soul. As our mind being renewed, other part of our soul, our our emotion, and our will follow... this transformation is an inward metabolic process of God's work."

24. **[Outline V] The believers being conformed to the image of God's firstborn Son — illustrated by every life (plant, animal) having its own essence and shape/form; the divine life's form in our spirit is the form of the firstborn Son of God.**
    > "Number five, The believers being conform to the image of God's first born son. The law of the spirit of life is operating us, conforming us to the image of the first born son of God... Every life has its uh essence and every life has if is shape... We have a divine life. And divine life has its essence. The divine life has its shape... What is the form of the divine life operating as the law in our spirit? This form is the form of the first born son of God."

25. **[Outline VI] The believers being saturated with the divine glory, the redemption of the body — when Christ returns believers receive full sonship, the body transfigured to be the same as Christ's glorious body, saturated with the glory of the Triune God.**
    > "Roman 6, is that right? The believers being saturated with the divine glory, the redemption of the body. Praise the Lord. Finally, our entire being will be saturated with the divine glory. This means that our body will be redeemed... our body, our body of sin, body of this death will be fully saturated with the glory of the Trion God."

26. **Recap: believers are presently in the process of sanctification, renewing, transformation, conformation, and glorification, with all the earlier negative deliverances (from the law of sin and death, the body of death, mortality, being common, low living, division) still operating alongside these positive processes.**
    > "Now we are in the process. We are in the process of what? Of sanctification. We are in the process of renewing. We are in the process of transformation. Process of confirmation and process of glorification... this law saves us from Satan's law of sin and of death. And this law saves us, delivers us from the body of this of body of this death. And this law also saves us from the mortality... And this law saves us from being common... This law saves us from the low manner of living. This law saves us from all kinds of division."

27. **Conclusion on how to enjoy this dispensing: turn to and exercise the human spirit — the crucial part of our being designed to contact, receive, and contain God — through prayer, calling on the Lord's name, and reading the word with a turned spirit.**
    > "Our human spirit is the crucial part of our being. Our human spirit was designed by God to contact God, to receive God, to contain God. So we need to turn to our spirit to receive more of God's dispensing. We can pray, we can call on the name of the Lord, we can read the word with the turn spirit."

28. **Additional teaching beyond the outline's core points: Romans 8:1-13 (the first half of the chapter) covers the law of the spirit of life; Romans 8:14-30 (the second half) covers the necessity of suffering for God's children before their full glorification.**
    > "One more thing, uh if you read Romans chapter 8, we are talk we have been talking this law of the spirit of life is about Romans uh Romans 8:1-13, the first half of the chapter. But the second half of the chapter verses 14-30, uh we there there is a necessity of suffering for God's children before their full glorification."

29. **Romans 8:28 — God sovereignly arranges all things (people, matters, events) to work together for good, meaning to transform and conform us to the image of God's Son — illustrated by Brother Lee's "hot oven" image (from the life-study of Genesis on Jacob) for our training environment, church life, and family life.**
    > "Romans 8:28 verse 28 says, 'And we know that all things work together for good. For those who love God.'... Good is... so that we may be transformed and conform to the image of God's son... In the life study of Genesis, if you look at the Jacob's transformation process, brother Lee said, 'God, into the hot oven.' Environment is a hot oven... Your church life is hot oven... family life, everything is hot oven."

30. **Romans 8:26 — the Spirit intercedes for us with groanings in our weakness; three parallel "growings" occur together — our own growing (v23), the Spirit's growing/groaning with us (v26), and all creation's groaning (v22) — waiting for the revelation of the sons of God, during which the Spirit dispenses the Triune God into us.**
    > "Paul says in Romans 8:26, moreover in like manner the spirit also joins in to help us in our weakness... the spirit himself intercedes for us with groaning which cannot be uttered... we have verse 23. In verse 23, it is our growing... And verse 26, it is the spirit's growing. And verse 22, the whole creation is growing as well... growing together for... the revelation of the sons of God."

31. **Closing exhortation: cooperate with the Triune God and the indwelling Spirit so as to be transformed, conformed, and eventually glorified, becoming the same as God in life and nature (but not in the Godhead), for the building up of the Body and the consummation of the New Jerusalem.**
    > "So, how what shall we do? We should cooperate with the Trion God. With we should cooperate with the indwelling indwelling spirit. So that we may be transformed and conform eventually so that we may be glorified... We will be the same as God in life and in nature, right? For the corporate expression of the triune God... Praise the Lord for the law of the spirit of life installed in us operating us to make us the same as God in life and in nature but not in the Godhead. For the building up of the body transformed into Jerusalem."
