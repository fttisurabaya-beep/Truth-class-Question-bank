**Source:** transcripts/raw/TRUTH_CLASS_JULDES_2026_R7_SENTUL_RONAL.txt (lines 403-816, section labeled "R7 - Truth 2 SENTUL (BR. RONAL)")
**Message (per Outline PDF):** Penyaluran Ilahi di dalam Kitab 1-2 Korintus (1) — Penyaluran Ilahi dari Kristus yang Almuhit dalam Menyuplai, Menunjang, Memberi Makan, Menyirami dan Mengubah Kaum Beriman
**Reference:** A Deeper Study of the Divine Dispensing, ch 8-9

## Points taught

Note on structure: line 407 (the very opening) is a devotional/worship-song passage ("Thank you...
mencari kidung 447 sungguh mulia sungguh aja Kunya Allah...") and is excluded below as preamble, not
teaching content. Line 411 begins the trainer's own illustration used to introduce the whole session
(the "box and treasure" story), which is included as point 1 because it functions as the trainer's
chosen entry point into the topic. As in message 7's Sentul instance, this trainer again spends
substantial time on background/bridging material (an 11-problems overview of 1 Corinthians, and a
"Christ and His cross" pivot) before formally opening the outline booklet around line 742, and again
runs out of time partway through — deferring outline point IX and never reaching 2 Corinthians (point
X) at all, consistent with the pattern already observed in message 7.

1. **[Trainer's own illustration] The "box and treasure" story — people often focus on the outer box (packaging) rather than the mustika (precious content) inside; likewise many do not perceive the treasure that is Christ, which is why they are indifferent or resistant when the gospel is brought to them — the concern is not whether Christ is precious, but whether people perceive His preciousness.**
   > "di dalam alkitab itu ada mustikanya Tetapi banyak orang Kristen bisa tidak fokus kepada mustikanya... Jeruk nggak pernah takut isinya... Waktu Tuhan Yesus lahir di dunia dia tidak pernah ketakutan untuk validasi manusia karena konten di dalamnya adalah apa adanya alat tritunggal yang kita khawatir adalah sedikit orang yang melihat nilai daripada Tuhan Yesus dari nazaret."

2. **Session topic announced: the Divine Dispensing of the all-inclusive Christ in the books of 1 and 2 Corinthians; the trainer first walks students through a "classic" overview of 1 Corinthians' eleven problems (numbered on a diagram) before turning to the outline itself — perpecahan/division (ch. 1-4), a wicked brother (ch. 5), lawsuits among believers, freedom in eating/drinking causing others to stumble (ch. 6), unequal marriage/divorce, food offered to idols (ch. 8-11, distinguished by two different treatments of the issue), head covering, the Lord's Supper abused, spiritual gifts (ch. 12-14, tongues vs. prophecy), the resurrection of the dead, and the collection for the saints.**
   > "eee bab pagi ini harusnya dua sesi ini kita membahas penyaluran dari Kristus yang Al muhit di dalam Kitab satu dan dua Korintus... kalau kita membaca Isinya banyak masalah ada berapa masalah di sana? 11 masalah kita gambar ya..."

3. **[Pivot, parallel to the SBY instance's point 2] The conclusion drawn from all eleven problems: Christ and His cross is the answer to every problem in the church — the focus in this training is not to be on the problems themselves but on Christ and Him crucified.**
   > "kitab satu Korintus ini Kristus dan salibnya adalah jawaban segala ya Kristus dan salibnya Apa itu adalah jawaban segala masalah dalam gereja... Tetapi kepada Kristus Kristus yang bagaimana Kristus yang Al muhit?"

4. **1 Kor. 2:2 — Paul's own resolve to know nothing except Jesus Christ and Him crucified is held up as a model resolution for the trainees: they must not let personal worries (roommate conflicts, work assignments, family problems, future/marriage anxieties) scatter their focus during this training, but should decide firmly to focus on Christ and His cross while still practically working through issues one at a time.**
   > "sebab aku telah memutuskan ini ya mutusin apa... Kristus dan salibnya adalah jawaban dari semua masalah yang ada di dalam gereja di Korintus maka itu pagi ini anda masuk ke pelatihan kan putuskan dengan benar putusanmu apa jangan kamu lihat masalah masalah di kamar masalah teman masalah di lapangan diperluasan masalah koordinasi masalah di rumah masalah keluarga."

5. **[Trainer's own illustration, explaining why dispensing requires death and resurrection] Nearness alone does not mean access to supply — just as living near Prabowo's Hambalang residence gives no relational benefit with the president, an unresurrected, glorious Christ in heaven "in Himself" would have no connection to us; it is specifically through His death and resurrection that He became the life-giving Spirit who can be dispensed into believers.**
   > "seperti hari ini Hambalang dekat sama kita ya Garuda yaksa itu dekat 10 menit dari sini tempat istananya Prabowo Prabowo sama kita enggak ada hubungannya... tetapi kan kita enggak menikmati penyaluran dia... Apalagi Kristus di surga nggak ada hubungan sama kita... melalui mati dan Bangkitlah dia menjadi roh pemberi hidup baru bisa disalurkan."

6. **[Outline I — 1 Kor. 1:2, 1:9, taught first from the Bible text before the outline booklet is formally opened] Christ as the common portion of all believers — all believers, despite whatever problems exist in a local church, are still God's church, sanctified in Christ Jesus, and have been called into the fellowship of God's Son; everyone shares equally in this divine portion because all have been called into this fellowship.**
   > "kepada jemaat Allah ini menarik sekali kalau ada satu jemaat yang banyak masalah begini kita rasanya berat ngomong jemaat Allah ini... Paulus clear ini Jemaat jemaat Allah dia enggak ubah di Korintus... kita semua ini berbagian di dalam satu penyaluran Kristus yang Al Muhid ini... kita semua sudah dipanggil Kok bisa semuanya berbagian dipanggil ke dalam persekutuan?"

7. **1 Kor. 6:17 — "he who is joined to the Lord is one spirit with Him"; joining oneself to the Lord (calling, praying, pondering the Word, praising, fellowshipping) is a practice, illustrated by electricity: fellowship with the Lord factually never disappears, but our experiential enjoyment of it can be "switched off" — not because the Lord withdraws, but because we have not "turned on" our own openness (per Rm. 9's vessel imagery).**
   > "tetapi siapa yang mengikatkan dirinya pada Tuhan menjadi satu roh dengan dia... persekutuan itu bisa lepas secara pengalamannya faktualnya nggak bisa tetapi dalam kenikmatannya dalam pengalaman bisa lepas seperti listrik... begitu dia di turn on anda tidak usah berusaha apa-apa listrik akan segera bersekutu dengan kita... kamu harus membuka Romo membuka hatimu karena kamu bejana menurut Roma 9."

8. **1 Kor. 12:13 — being baptized in one Spirit into one Body, and all being made to drink of one Spirit — this baptism is itself an act of dispensing, applied not only into the Trinity but into one Body; every believer without exception is included ("dibaptis dikatakan satu tubuh diberi minum dari satu roh").**
   > "sebab dalam satu roh kita semua baik orang Yahudi maupun orang Yunani baik budak maupun orang Merdeka telah dibaptis menjadi satu tubuh dan kita semua diberi minum dari satu roh... dibaptis juga soal penyaluran bukan saja dibaptis ke dalam Bapak Putra Roh Kudus tetapi ke dalam satu tubuh."

9. **1 Kor. 15:58 — "stand firm, be unmovable, always abounding in the Lord's work, because your labor is not in vain in the Lord"; this fellowship with the Lord and with the Body is what steadies and motivates believers so that ministry labor does not become wearisome or unmotivated.**
   > "Karena itu saudara-saudaraku yang kekasih berdirilah Teguh Jangan goyah, giatlah selalu dalam pekerjaan tuhan, sebab kamu tahu dalam persekutuan dengan Tuhan jerih payamu tidak sia-sia... Kristus dan salibnya itu adalah jawaban bersekutu dengan Tuhan bersekutu dengan saudara-saudari dalam tubuh ikatkan Dirimu Satu roh terima penyaluran Ilahi."

10. **[Outline formally opened, re-covering point I with an addition] 1 Kor. 1:7-8 is added to outline point I: believers "do not lack in any gift" while waiting for the revelation of the Lord Jesus Christ — whatever sense of lack a believer feels, there is a continuous divine portion from the all-inclusive Christ being dispensed while they wait.**
   > "penyaluran Kristus yang Al muhit dalam menyuplai menopang memberi makan menyiram dan mentranformasi kaum beriman romawi 1... demikianlah pasal 1 ayat 7 demikianlah kamu tidak kekurangan dalam dalam suatu karunia pun sementara kamu menantikan pernyataan Tuhan kita Yesus Kristus... selama kita nunggu ini ada porsi Ilahi dari Syekh Al Muhid yang terus disalurkan kamu enggak kurang kok."

11. **[Outline II — 1 Kor. 1:24a] Christ as the power and wisdom of God for those who are called; ayat 30 is then read in preview, listing Christ as becoming wisdom, righteousness, sanctification, and redemption to us — righteousness as past, sanctification as present ("saat ini kita sedang dikuduskan"), redemption as future (glorification).**
   > "Kristus adalah kekuatan Allah dan hikmat Allah Oke ayat 24 kekuatan dan nikmat Allah ayat 30 nya juga bagus ya, tetapi oleh dia kamu berada dalam Kristus Yesus yang oleh Allah telah menjadi Hikmat bagi kita membenarkan menguduskan dan menebus kita ini juga bagus sekali... ini adalah ini present ini adalah future."

12. **[Outline III — wisdom, 1 Kor. 1:24b, 30] Marked only as already covered via the ayat 30 preview above, without any separate elaboration of III.A (memberikan nasihat) or III.B (melengkapi kaum saleh dengan taktik).**
    > "Romawi yang ketiga sama-sama Kristus Ilahi sebagai hikmat Ya udah ini udah kita cover di sini ya ayat 30 ya."

13. **[Outline IV — righteousness, 1 Kor. 1:30] Named only in passing, again pointed back to ayat 30, and explicitly assigned as self-study rather than taught in session; no elaboration of IV.A (prosedur dan metode) or IV.B (benar dan tepat terhadap Allah, manusia, segala sesuatu) is given.**
    > "romawi ke 4 saudara Kristus yang Al muhit sebagai penyaluran Ilahi kebenaran dari Allah Oke, Saya kira Anda bisa pelajari dulu pelajari lagi sendiri outlinenya masih di ayat 30 ya."

14. **[Outline VI — redemption, 1 Kor. 1:30 — reached directly from IV, skipping outline V (pengudusan) as a separate point] All of these (wisdom, righteousness, sanctification, redemption in ayat 30) only become experiential reality through dispensing, illustrated by a building's electrical installation (AC, lighting, sound system) that does not manifest until the switch is turned on; the practical "switch" is binding oneself to the Lord every morning, keeping the spirit active even while seated in class.**
    > "Kristus yang Al Muhid Oke baik semua di dalam ayat yang ke-30, Tetapi semua itu bisa berjalan Kalau ada ayat ini kalau tidak ada ayat ini ini belum jadi pengalaman kita... sama seperti gedung ini ketika dibangun di instalasikan listriknya... Tetapi semua itu tidak manifest kalau Belum dihidupin saklarnya... jadi saklarnya apa diikatkan dirimu kepada Tuhan pagi hari."

15. **[Outline VIII — spiritual rock and bread from heaven, 1 Kor. 10:3-4; Jn. 6 — reached directly after VI, skipping outline VII (Passover Lamb and unleavened bread) entirely] Israel was baptized in the sea and cloud through Moses and drank from a spiritual rock that "followed them" — that rock was Christ; this is supplemented with John 6's bread of life from heaven, so that believers have both living water and the bread of life so as not to go hungry.**
    > "Kristus yang Al Muhid sebagai penyaluran Ilahi sebagai batu karang rohani yang dari yang mengalir keluar Oke ditambahkan Yohanes 6 ya tetapi satu Korintus 10 di sini... mereka minum dari batu karang, tetapi Alkitab jelas mengatakan batu karang itu adalah Kristus karena penyelamat bilang batu karang yang mengikuti mereka... ada juga tambahan ya Yohanes pasal 6 roti yang turun dari surga roti Hayat lah."

16. **[Outline IX — anointing and sealing, 2 Kor. 1:21-22 — explicitly deferred, not taught] The trainer names outline point IX but immediately defers all of 2 Corinthians to the next session, ending the substantive teaching there.**
    > "Romawi yang terakhir eh bukan terakhir ya ke berapa ini 9 sama-sama dibaca Kristus ya Al muhit Oke ini nanti di sesi berikutnya aja lah 2 Korintus ini ya sudah ya kan Baik saya sampai di sini dulu."

17. **[Closing exhortation] Reiterates that focus must remain on Christ and His cross rather than on problems; encourages the sisters toward prayer and fellowship as the channel that brings divine dispensing into church life, and the brothers toward carrying leadership well (head-covering/order) and toward being "one step ahead" in anticipating upcoming issues (illustrated by a pianist who must already be thinking of the next chord); closes with an admonition not to treat morning devotion, Bible reading, and prayer as mere obligation but as the very channel through which the Spirit and life are dispensed.**
    > "fokusmu bukan melihat masalah tetapi kepada Kristus dan salibnya... doa itu akan membawakan Kristus sebagai penudungan kepala kita yang baik... pemain musik itu biasanya harusnya pintar karena dia one step ahead... Jangan remehkan penyegaran pagimu jangan remehkan baca alkitabmu Jangan remehkan doamu... kalau kita punya Konsep ini ada penyaluran nih mau dimasukkan ke dalam kita dan yang disalurkan itu bukan benda lain Kristus dan salibnya ini loh yang Al muhit ini yang akan menjadi jawaban semua persoalan."

## Terminology used

Cross-checked against the Outline PDF's message 8 section — ID: "Berita Delapan — Penyaluran Ilahi
di dalam Kitab 1-2 Korintus (1) — Penyaluran Ilahi dari Kristus yang Almuhit dalam Menyuplai,
Menunjang, Memberi Makan, Menyirami dan Mengubah Kaum Beriman" (`Outline Truth 1st Year 2023
(ID).pdf`, p. 11-12), read directly via `pdftotext -layout` for this task.

- **Kristus yang Al muhit / Al Muhid / Al Muhit** — used throughout (e.g. lines 611, 665, 750, 778), matching the outline's recurring header phrase "Kristus yang almuhit." The transcript's ASR renders it inconsistently as "Al muhit," "Al Muhid," and once "Syekh Al Muhid" (line 758) — clearly the same term mis-transcribed, not a distinct concept.
- **menyuplai, menopang/menunjang, memberi makan, menyiram dan mentransformasi/mengubah kaum beriman** — line 750 ("penyaluran Kristus yang Al muhit dalam menyuplai menopang memberi makan menyiram dan mentranformasi kaum beriman") reproduces the outline's own subtitle almost verbatim, confirming the trainer reads directly from this outline title once it is opened (around line 742-750).
- **kekayaan Allah di dalam Kristus** — outline I: "menyuplaikan kekayaan Allah dalam Kristus kepada mereka." Line 665 and 750 both use this near-verbatim.
- **dipanggil ke dalam persekutuan** — outline I.B ("Allah telah memanggil kaum beriman ke dalam Kristus," 1 Kor. 1:9). Line 665-673 closely echoes this, using "persekutuan" (fellowship) rather than "ke dalam Kristus" specifically — a minor wording variant of the same idea, reinforced by the outline's own I.A "dinikmati bersama."
- **kekuatan Allah dan hikmat Allah** — outline II/III header (1 Kor. 1:24). Line 766 quotes 1 Kor. 1:24 directly; matches.
- **membenarkan, menguduskan dan menebus** — outline III/IV/V/VI (via 1 Kor. 1:30). Line 766 quotes this triad verbatim from the Bible text, but — unlike the SBY instance, which elaborated each of III, IV, V, VI as separate outline points with their own A/B sub-points — this trainer treats the triad only as a single preview quotation and explicitly defers or skips detailed treatment of III, IV, and V (see "Not covered" below).
- **taktik** (outline III.B, "melengkapi kaum saleh dengan taktik") — not used at all in this instance; III is only marked as "already covered" via the ayat 30 preview, with no elaboration of either III.A or III.B.
- **prosedur dan metode** (outline IV.A) — not used; consistent with the SBY instance, where this same sub-point (IV.A) was also the one clear gap, this instance likewise never mentions "prosedur" or "metode."
- **agar roh, jiwa, dan tubuh kaum beriman dapat dikuduskan seluruhnya** (outline V.B) — not used at all; outline point V (pengudusan/sanctification) is skipped as a distinct topic altogether in this instance (the trainer moves from IV directly to VI), a stronger gap than in the SBY instance, which did cover V in full.
- **Anak Domba Paskah dan roti yang tidak beragi** (outline VII, 1 Kor. 5:7-8) — not used at all; outline point VII is skipped entirely (the trainer moves from VI directly to VIII), unlike the SBY instance, which covered VII (with some ASR garbling of "beragi").
- **batu karang rohani... yang mengikuti mereka** / **roti dari surga** — outline VIII header (1 Kor. 10:3-4; Yoh. 6:57b-58a). Line 778 reproduces this imagery closely, including the explicit citation of "Yohanes pasal 6" — matches the SBY instance's similarly close treatment of this point.
- **pengurapan Roh majemuk, dan... meterai** (outline IX, 2 Kor. 1:21-22) — the term "pengurapan"/"meterai" is never actually used in this transcript; outline IX is only named ("Romawi yang terakhir... ke berapa ini 9") and then immediately deferred to a future session, with no content taught.
- **tulisan tinta... cermin** (outline X, 2 Kor. 3:3, 18) — not used at all; 2 Corinthians is not reached in this instance.
- **11 masalah di Korintus** — the trainer's own detailed background walkthrough of all eleven problems in 1 Corinthians (points 2 above), paralleling the SBY instance's much briefer mention of "11 masalah" (SBY point 1) but far more elaborated here — not itself outline terminology for message 8's I-X points, but background/bridging material as in the SBY instance's own point 1 and point 3.
- **kotak dan mustika** (box and treasure) and the **Hambalang/istana Prabowo** illustration — both are the trainer's own original illustrations, not found in the outline text, used respectively to open the session and to explain why Christ's death/resurrection (not mere heavenly glory) is what makes Him dispensable.
- **listrik/saklar** (electricity/switch) illustration — used twice, once for 1 Kor. 6:17 (fellowship's factual permanence vs. experiential availability) and again for outline VI (ayat 30's blessings not manifesting until "the switch is turned on") — the trainer's own recurring illustration, not outline text.
- **Genuinely unclear/garbled passages flagged (not guessed at):** line 407, the opening devotional/song passage, contains several English/Indonesian code-switched fragments that do not form coherent sentences ("Can you want Can you Allah konsisten logis tapi you next come to you nose as Empress give to me your speed we need aspek") — almost certainly ASR mis-transcription of a worship song or prayer in mixed language; excluded from Points taught and not interpreted here. Line 758's "Syekh Al Muhid" is very likely a mis-transcription of "Kristus yang Al Muhit" (possibly picking up a nearby word as "Syekh"); treated as the same term as elsewhere, not a new concept.

## Not covered in this instance

Outline structure for message 8 (I-X, confirmed via direct `pdftotext -layout` read of the Indonesian

- **VII.** Kristus sebagai Anak Domba Paskah dan roti tidak beragi — 1 Kor. 5:7-8 — **NOT covered at all.** The trainer moves directly from outline VI to outline VIII, skipping VII entirely; no reference to Passover, the lamb, or unleavened bread appears anywhere in lines 403-816. - **VIII.** Kristus sebagai batu karang rohani dan roti dari surga — 1 Kor. 10:3-4; Yoh. 6:57b-58a — covered (point 15; close paraphrase plus explicit "Yohanes pasal 6" citation, comparable in depth to the SBY instance's treatment).
- **IX.** Kristus sebagai pengurapan Roh majemuk dan meterai — 2 Kor. 1:21-22 — **NOT covered.** The trainer explicitly defers this and all of 2 Corinthians to "sesi berikutnya" (point 16); no content is taught.
- **X.** Kristus sebagai tulisan tinta dan cermin — 2 Kor. 3:3, 18 — **NOT covered.** As with IX, 2 Corinthians is never reached in this instance.

**Summary:** this instance covers noticeably less of the message 8 outline than the SBY instance,
and in a different pattern than message 7's Sentul instance (which spent most of its time on a
whole-book recap before reaching only a few outline points). Here, the trainer opens with two of his
own illustrations (the box/treasure story and the Hambalang/Prabowo analogy) plus a detailed
walkthrough of 1 Corinthians' eleven problems (points 1-5) as a runway into the topic, then teaches
outline points **I, II, VI, and VIII** with reasonable depth (each anchored by a direct Bible-text
quotation and, for VI, an original illustration), touches **III and IV only in passing** (both
explicitly pointed back to a single preview quotation of 1 Kor. 1:30 rather than taught individually,
with IV assigned as self-study), and **skips V and VII entirely** without comment — a stronger gap
than either point missing from the SBY instance, which covered both V and VII in full. **IX is
explicitly deferred** to a future session ("sesi berikutnya"), and **X and all of 2 Corinthians are
never reached.** As with message 7's Sentul instance, this again reflects a time-constrained session:
substantial class time goes to background/bridging material and the trainer's own illustrations before
the outline booklet is formally opened around line 742, leaving less than half of the outline's ten
points (I, II, VI, VIII fully; III, IV only nominally) taught with real depth by the time the session
ends.
