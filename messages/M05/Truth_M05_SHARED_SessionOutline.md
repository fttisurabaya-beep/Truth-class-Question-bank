## Points taught

1. **Transition into message 5's thesis: in Romans, the processed Triune God operates as the law of the spirit of life, dispensing divine life into the believer's tripartite being (spirit, soul, body).**
   > "So in Paul's writings we see the full of the dispensing of the divine Trinity and we see in Paul's epistles we see his processes is becoming the life giving spirit especially in Romans chapter He is working. He is operating as the law of the spirit of life. Dispensing his divine life into every part of our being."

2. **The term "spirit of life" is found in Romans 8:2; it is the compound Spirit, the all-inclusive compound Spirit.**
   > "It is in Romans chapter 8:2... this Roman numeral talks about the spirit of life is what is the compound spirit. The spirit of life, the life-giving spirit is the all-inclusive Amen. man compound spirit."

3. **The compound Spirit is typified in Exodus 30's holy anointing oil (Exo 30:23-25), a mingling of the divinity, humanity, human living, death and resurrection of Christ with the Spirit of God.**
   > "And this is typified in in where? In Exodus 30... being a mingling of the divinity, humanity, human living, death and resurrection of Christ with the spirit of God to become the holy ointment."

4. **The Exodus 30 recipe was read aloud: one hin of olive oil compounded with four spices (myrrh, cinnamon, calamus, cassia) into a holy anointing oil.**
   > "You also take this finest spices of flowing mer 500 shekel and of fragrant cinnamon half as much, 250 shekels, and a fragrant calamus, 250 shekels, and of cassia, 500 shekels... and a hen of olive oil, and you shall make it a holy anointing oil... So, here in this verse we see a holy anointing oil. It is an ointment... composed of one tin of olive oil plus with four four spices."

5. **The olive oil represents the divinity of Christ / the Holy Spirit.**
   > "The basic ingredient of this uh holy anointing oil is olive oil, right?... This represents what? The divinity of of Christ... In the Bible, olive oil always represents the Holy Spirit."

6. **The four spices each carry a specific meaning: myrrh = the sweet death of Christ; cinnamon = the effectiveness of that death; calamus = the resurrection of Christ; cassia = the power of Christ's resurrection; the number four signifies creation/humanity.**
   > "Myrrh signifies what? Signifies the sweet death of Christ. And cinnamon signifies the sweet the effectiveness of the death of Christ and what about Kalamas? Kalamas uh signifies the resurrection of Christ and Casia it signifies the power of Christ's resurrection... Number four in the Bible signifies what? The creation, the humanity."

7. **The supplying element of this compound Spirit is bountiful and excellent (Phil 1:19) — called there "the spirit of Jesus Christ" with its "bountiful supply," illustrated by the chorus/Koregos (chorus-leader) who supplies all needs of the choir.**
   > "So, the spirit in Philippians in Philippians 1:19, we see the term the spirit of Jesus Christ... in this verse, we have the term the bountiful supply... It refers to the supplying of all the needs of the chorus in the ancient time by the leader by the Corregus, the leader of the chorus."

8. **The bountiful supply of the spirit of Jesus Christ comes to us through the Body — enjoying it requires being in the Body.**
   > "It means that this bountiful supply of the spirit of Jesus Christ is coming to us through the body. So, when you like to enjoy the bountiful supply of the spirit, you need to be in the body."

9. **The law of the spirit of life is the consummated Triune God (Romans 8).**
   > "In Roman 2, it says the law of the spirit of life being the consummated trium God... And this law of the spirit of life is what? Is the consummated Triune God."

10. **Romans 7-8 contain four laws; the first is the law of good in the mind, present in every human because made in God's image.**
    > "If you read Romans chapters, Romans chapter 7 and 8, there are four laws, four laws. Number one, there is the law of good in our mind, law of good... every human being has a law of good."

11. **The second law is the law of sin in the flesh (entering after the fall); Paul described the struggle between the law of good and the law of sin, and being defeated by the law of sin.**
    > "There is another law. This is called the law of sin in man's flesh... there was another law in our flesh. And this law is called the the law of sin. So, Paul was expressing there was a struggle. Struggle between the law of good and the law of sin."

12. **The third law is the law of God (the Ten Commandments), whose purpose is to expose man's true condition and reveal that God is a God of love, light, holiness, and righteousness — yet the law of sin always conquers when man tries to keep it.**
    > "That is the law of God. Law of God. This is represented by the Ten Commandments... this law reveals that God is a God of love. God is a God of light. God is a God of holiness and righteousness. But whenever we uh try to keep this law of God because there is a law of sin in our flesh. This law of sin always conquer us."

13. **The fourth law is the law of the spirit of life, found only in believers in the Lord Jesus — this law is the Triune God Himself, installed in every Christian.**
    > "Praise the Lord, we have the fourth law, which is called the law of the spirit of life. This law of the spirit of life is found only in those who have believed in the Lord Jesus. This law is the Triune God himself... this law has been installed in every one of us."

14. **The law of life is the innate, automatic function of the divine life — illustrated by the peach tree's law of bearing peaches and the dog's law of barking, which need no teaching.**
    > "What is the law of life. It is the innate automatic function of the divine life... If a farmer uh teach a peach tree saying... my desire is to is for you to bring forth peach. [The tree replies] Don't worry. I am history. I have the life of history... So, we don't need to teach the dog to bark."

15. **The airplane illustration: being in Christ means we don't need to strive or fly ourselves — Christ, as the law of the spirit of life installed in us, is the one operating.**
    > "If we are in Christ, we don't need to try. We don't need to strive to improve ourselves, right? Because this Christ has the law of the spirit of life and this spirit of life has been installed in our being... It is Christ who is the airplane who is flying."

16. **Rather than praying for victory or revival by striving, believers should call on the name of the Lord and praise/sing — as Paul and Silas did in prison (Acts 16).**
    > "Many times, Christians they pray to the Lord for victory. They pray to the Lord for revival... this is like trying to fly by ourselves... you just praise the Lord. You need to sing. Call on the name of the... It's like Apostle Paul and his co-worker Silas in chapter 16 of Acts, right? When they put into jail, what they what did they do? They were singing."

17. **The law of the spirit of life frees believers from the law of sin and of death, solving the problem of sin and death — the method is not resolutions but calling on the Lord and contacting the spirit so the law is activated.**
    > "This law of the spirit of life frees us from the law of sin and from the law of death, solving for us the problem of sin and of death... The method to overcome these problems is not make any resolutions, right? We need to call on the Lord. We need to contact the spirit. We need to let the law of the spirit of life be activated, living, operating in our spirit."

18. **The divine dispensing begins from the believer's spirit: Christ enters the spirit by believing/calling on His name and makes the spirit alive — the spirit becomes Zoe (divine life).**
    > "Okay, Roman 3 says beginning from the believers spirit... First, Christ enters into our spirit, right? By believing into him by calling on his name. Christ enters into our spirit and makes our spirit alive... it is a divine life. It is Zoe, right? So our spirit is Zoe."

19. **Romans 8:9 shows this is the initial point of the Triune God's dispensing: God's Spirit dwelling in the believers means we are not in the flesh but in the spirit.**
    > "Romans 8:9 says, 'You are not in the flesh, but in the spirit. If indeed the spirit of God dwells in you.'... this is the initial point of the Triune God dispensing within us. It begins with our spirit by the dwelling of the spirit of God in our spirit."

20. **The Spirit of God, the Spirit of Christ, and Christ Himself (the pneumatic Christ) are one and the same, actively making home in the believer's spirit, infusing life and righteousness into it (Rom 8:9-10).**
    > "We have the first, we have the spirit of God, then we have the spirit of Christ, and then we have Christ himself, who is numerically Christ. So, all this all three are one, right?... This spirit actively makes his home in our spirit, infusing life, righteousness into our spirit, making our spirit life."

21. **Romans 8:10 — though the body is dead because of sin, the believer's spirit becomes life because of righteousness; the spirit is not merely living, it IS life, a fact to be declared regardless of feeling.**
    > "The believers though dead in their bodies because of sin may have their spirits become life because of righteousness... our spirit is not merely living. Our spirit is life. How about we shout, our spirit is life... It's not according to your feeling... It is the word of God."

22. **The divine dispensing continues into the believer's soul (Romans 4 in the trainer's own numbering) by the believer setting the mind of the soul on the spirit — Romans 8:6, mind on the flesh is death, mind on the spirit is life and peace.**
    > "The divine dispensing is continuing in the believer's soul. This is Roman 4... by the believer setting the mind of their soul on the spirit... It says, 'For the mind set on the flesh is death but the mindset on the spirit is life and peace.'"

23. **The mind is neutral and is the battlefield of the warfare between spirit and flesh; the will must be used to set the mind on the spirit to gain victory.**
    > "Our mind is neutral. It is between flesh and on the spirit... There is a warfare between our spirit and the flesh. So, where what is the battlefield? Where is the battlefield? Our mind. Our mind is the battlefield of the warfare of our spirit and flesh... We need to, you know, use our will to set our mind on the spirit. Then you can gain victory."

24. **The dispensing dwelling in the spirit spreads into the soul so the mind is transformed and the soul renewed (Romans 12:2); the mind is the leading part of the soul, and will and emotion follow the mind.**
    > "That divine dispensing that dwells in the believer's spirit may spread into the believer's soul that their mind may be transformed and their soul renewed. Romans 12:2 says, do not be fashioned according to this age, but be transformed by the renewing of the mind... our will and our emotion will be renewed also... because our mind is the leading part."

25. **Being renewed means a new element — God Himself as the life-giving Spirit — is added into our being, producing an inward metabolic transformation.**
    > "There's a new element in the universe. Nothing new under the heaven. Only God, right? Only God himself who has become the life-giving spirit. He is the new element... So this produces a inward metabolic transformation. So we are being transformed by the renewing of the mind."

26. **The divine dispensing is consummated in the believer's body (Romans 5 in the trainer's numbering); Romans 8:11 reveals three matters: the entire Triune God, the process required for the dispensing (implied in "Jesus," "Christ," and "raised"), and the dispensing itself, shown by "give life to your mortal bodies."**
    > "And this divine dispensing will be consummated in the believer's body... Romans 8:11 says... in this verse we have three matters. First, we have the entire Triune God... number two, We have the process required for his dispensing... number third, we have what? We have is dispensing... shown by the phrase, give life to your mortal bodies."

27. **"Give life to your mortal bodies" does not refer to divine healing but to being enlivened to carry out God's will — illustrated by a brother suffering from cancer who still exercises his spirit and prophesies in the meetings.**
    > "This does not refer to the divine healing. God may heal us and he may not... It refers to the resulting of our allowing the spirit of God to make his home in us and saturate our entire being with his life... his mortal body, right? His dying body is being used by the Lord... he is prophesying."

28. **As a result, the mortal body receives the supply and saturation of the divine life, in contrast to the fallen body, which is called the body of sin and the body of death.**
    > "The believer's mortal body may also receive the supply and saturation of the divine life... Our fallen body is called what? Called the flesh of sin, right? the body of body of sin and the body of this death, right? But our physical body now our physical body can partake of the divine life."

29. **Summary: the law of the spirit of life is an organic principle that functions automatically (like the law of digestion), dispensing the riches of the Triune God from spirit to mind to body — the result is that believers become sons of God, members of the Body of Christ, and are conformed and glorified toward the consummation of the New Jerusalem.**
    > "It is the organic principle that functions automatically. So, through the working of this law, the essence, the element, the riches of the Triune God are being dispensed into our being... as a result, through enjoying this divine dispensing, in Romans, we become the sons of God, right? We become some members of the body of Christ... and we can be fully fully conform and glorified to to consummate the new Jerusalem."
